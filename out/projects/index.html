The Charcoal Corner

Beyond logic and code, there lies Art

Outside of my professional life..

I'm a natural artist with a self-taught love for sketching, drawn to the raw expression that charcoal allows. I specialize in portraits that focus on realism, emotion, and intricate facial details. Art has instilled in me patience, depth, and a sense of creative flow.

This artistic perspective shapes how I approach UI development. I tend to see layout, spacing, and visual harmony the way I view a blank sketchpad, with intention and balance. It's more than a hobby, it's a quiet anchor in my life. In this space, I share a few pieces that hold something personal.
